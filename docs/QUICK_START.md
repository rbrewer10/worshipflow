# WorshipFlow Pro — Quick Start for Sunday Volunteers

A one-page guide for running slides at Snow Hill Church. Keep this taped by the booth computer.

---

## 1. Opening the app

Double-click WorshipFlow Pro to open it. You'll land on the **Home** screen, which shows:

- A big green **Go live** button at the top (jumps straight to Live control)
- A row of cards: **Zone screens**, **Stage monitor**, **Build service**, **Song library**, **Scripture**, **Volunteer mode**
- A left-hand sidebar you can use any time to jump between sections

If a service is already loaded, the Go live button will show its name and how many items are in it.

## 2. Pick or build today's service

1. Click **Build service** in the sidebar (or the card on Home).
2. On the left you'll see a list of saved services. Click one to open it.
3. Don't have one yet? Type a name in the box at the top of that list and click the **+** button to create a new one, then add songs, scripture, or announcement slides to it.
4. If someone already built the service on another computer, use **Load saved service (.wfservice)** to bring it in.

Once a service is open, it's ready to go live.

## 3. Go live with the first song or item

1. Click **Go live** on Home, or **Live control** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see two places to start an item:
   - **Left column (service list):** click an item once — it turns amber and says "tap to cancel." Leave it alone for about a second and a half and it goes live automatically. This little pause is on purpose, so you don't accidentally send the wrong song to the screen with a stray click. Tap it again during that window to cancel.
   - **Middle area (slide thumbnails):** click any individual slide thumbnail and it goes live **immediately** — no confirmation pause. Use this when you already know exactly which slide you want.
3. Once something is live, it's on the projector / stage screens right away.

## 4. Moving to the next slide during the service

Once you're live, you don't need to keep clicking thumbnails. Use these while the booth computer window is focused (not typing in a text box):

- **Spacebar** or the **Right Arrow (→)** key — advance to the next slide
- **Left Arrow (←)** key — go back to the previous slide

You can also click directly on any slide thumbnail in the middle of the Live screen to jump straight to it.

## 5. Blanking the screen (Black)

Need the screen dark between songs, during a prayer, or during an awkward pause? Press **B** on your keyboard, or click the black **Black** button in the top-right panel of the Live screen. Press it again (or send Lyrics/Logo) to bring the screen back.

## 6. Showing the church logo

To put up the Snow Hill logo screen (for example, during the welcome or between service segments), press **L**, or click the **Logo** button next to Black in the top-right panel.

To get back to normal slides/lyrics, press **S**, or click the green **Live** button.

## 7. Sending a quick note to the stage

If the pastor or worship leader needs a heads-up (running long, speak up, move to the closing song, etc.) without you walking up front:

1. In Live control, find the **Stage Message** box in the right-hand panel.
2. Click one of the **Quick Messages** buttons (e.g. "5 minutes left," "Slow down," "Pray now") to send it instantly — it appears on the stage monitor.
3. Or type your own message in the box and click **Send**.
4. Click **Clear** to remove the message from the stage screen when it's no longer needed.

## 8. Volunteer mode — the simple screen

If tonight's operator is new or just needs the basics, click **Volunteer mode** in the sidebar (or the card on Home). This switches to a stripped-down, big-button screen with:

- **Black** and **Logo** buttons at the top
- Giant **Prev** and **Next** arrows on the left and right — most of the screen is just those two buttons
- A strip along the bottom to jump straight to any item in the service
- A dropdown at the top to switch which service is loaded

All the same keyboard shortcuts (Space/→ next, ← previous, B black, L logo) still work in Volunteer mode. Click **Exit** in the top corner to return to the full interface.

Use Volunteer mode any time you want someone to be able to run the service without needing to understand the rest of the app — it's basically foolproof.

## 9. If something looks wrong

- **Wrong slide is showing:** Click the correct slide's thumbnail directly in the Live screen (middle area) — it goes live immediately, no need to wait.
- **Projector shows nothing / black:** Check the **Displays** info under "More" in the right-hand panel of Live control — it tells you how many screens are connected and how many are "live." If it says 0 live, try sending **Logo** or **Live** again with the buttons or L/S keys.
- **You're not sure what's live:** Look at the small preview at the bottom of the left-hand service list in Live control — it mirrors exactly what's on the big screen right now.
- **Stuck or unsure who to click:** Switch to **Volunteer mode** — it removes the extra options and leaves just Prev/Next/Black/Logo, which is hard to get wrong.
- **Still stuck:** Find the tech lead. Don't panic — Black is always your safe button if you need a moment to sort things out.
